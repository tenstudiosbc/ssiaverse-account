TSBC Games Transparency Commitment

Privacy Policy

SSIAVerse Account Services

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

At TSBC Games Division, a division of TenStudios Broadcasting Community, we believe that trust is the foundation of every great gaming experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and protect your personal information when you create and use a SSIAVerse Account — your gateway to the SSIAVerse universe, including titles such as SSIA Vanguard Chronicles and SSIA ShadowFront.

We are committed to TSBC Games Transparency: we will always tell you what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and how we protect it. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us using the contact information provided at the end of this document.

1. Information We Collect

When you create and use a SSIAVerse Account, we may collect and process:

  • Email Address: The email address you provide when creating your account. SSIAVerse currently uses Firebase Email/Password Authentication and requires a valid @gmail.com address for web account registration and authentication.
  • Firebase Authentication User ID (UID): Firebase generates a unique identifier for your authenticated account. This UID is used internally to associate your account with your SSIAVerse data.
  • Username / Account Identifier: Your account identifier used within the SSIAVerse ecosystem. For the current web account system, the authenticated Gmail address is used as the web-side account mapping identifier.
  • Account Profile Information: Information such as your display name, account email address, and optional profile information such as a biography when provided through account settings.
  • Game Progress & Preferences: Save data, game-related settings, linked-game information, and other gameplay information associated with your account.
  • Account Activity Information: Where applicable, account-related activity information may be stored for security, operational, and support purposes.
Password Security: SSIAVerse does not store your plain-text password in Firestore. Password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. Your password is not stored by SSIAVerse as a custom password hash.

We do not intentionally collect your real-world name, phone number, physical address, payment information, or government-issued identification through the SSIAVerse Account system unless you voluntarily provide such information through a separate support interaction or another service.

2. How We Collect Your Information

SSIAVerse uses Firebase Authentication and Firebase Firestore, services provided by Google, as part of its account and backend infrastructure.

SSIAVerse currently uses Email/Password Authentication for web accounts. This means:

  • You provide a valid @gmail.com address when creating or accessing your SSIAVerse web account.
  • Firebase Authentication creates and manages the authentication account associated with your email address.
  • Firebase generates a unique User ID (UID) for your authenticated account.
  • SSIAVerse uses that Firebase UID to associate your account profile and web account data with your authenticated identity.
  • Game-related information is stored in Firestore according to the account and game data structures used by SSIAVerse.
  • Data transmitted between your device and Firebase services is protected through encrypted connections.

3. Password & Authentication Security

Important: SSIAVerse no longer implements its own client-side password hashing or password-storage system. Password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.

When you sign in to SSIAVerse using your Gmail address and password, the authentication process is handled through Firebase Authentication. SSIAVerse's Firestore database does not store a custom passwordHash for your account.

This architecture allows Firebase Authentication to handle the authentication credential while SSIAVerse uses the resulting Firebase UID to associate the authenticated account with its Firestore data.

Password Reset

If you forget your password, SSIAVerse uses Firebase Authentication's password-reset functionality to send a password-reset email to the email address associated with your Firebase account.

SSIAVerse does not generate or store its own password-reset password hashes. Password-reset functionality is handled through Firebase Authentication.

Password Changes

When you change your password through SSIAVerse account settings, the website uses Firebase Authentication to perform the password change. Firebase may require you to authenticate again before performing sensitive account operations.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for purposes associated with operating, maintaining, securing, and supporting SSIAVerse Account Services.

  • Authentication: To authenticate your SSIAVerse Account and allow you to sign in.
  • Account Management: To maintain your account profile and account settings.
  • Game Synchronization: To store and synchronize supported game progress, saves, settings, and related information.
  • Linked Games: To associate supported SSIAVerse games with your account.
  • Security: To protect account data and enforce access controls.
  • Support: To investigate account-related problems and respond to support requests.

We do not use your account data for: targeted advertising, selling personal information, building behavioral profiles for marketing, or unrelated commercial purposes.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

TSBC Games Division does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

SSIAVerse uses Google Firebase as a third-party infrastructure provider. Firebase provides services including Firebase Authentication and Firestore, which are used to operate SSIAVerse Account Services.

As part of providing these services, information associated with your account may be processed by Google according to the applicable Firebase and Google service terms, privacy practices, and security policies.

We do not intentionally share SSIAVerse account information with advertisers, analytics companies, social media platforms, or unrelated game studios unless required by law, necessary to protect our legal rights, or necessary to operate a service you have requested.

6. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your SSIAVerse Account remains active or as otherwise necessary to provide the services associated with your account.

If your account is inactive for an extended period, including potentially 24 months or more, TSBC Games Division may review the account for possible deletion in accordance with our operational practices. Where appropriate, we may provide notice before permanently deleting inactive account data.

You may also request deletion of your SSIAVerse Account and associated account data at any time, subject to any information that we may be required to retain for legal or security purposes.

7. Security Measures

TSBC Games Division implements multiple layers of security to protect SSIAVerse Account information.

  • Firebase Authentication: Authentication credentials are handled through Firebase Authentication rather than being stored as custom passwords in Firestore.
  • Firebase Security Rules: Firestore access rules restrict protected account and game data according to the authenticated Firebase UID and the ownership relationships defined by the SSIAVerse backend.
  • UID-Based Account Ownership: Web account data is associated with the authenticated Firebase UID rather than relying solely on a user-provided username.
  • Game-Specific Ownership: Supported game data is separated by game identifier, with ownership checks applied to protected save data.
  • Encrypted Transmission: Data transmitted between your device and Firebase services is protected through encrypted network connections.
  • Account Reauthentication: Firebase may require recent authentication before allowing sensitive operations such as changing or deleting account credentials.
  • Security Reviews: We periodically review our account architecture and Firebase configuration to identify and address potential security weaknesses.
Security Transparency: While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should maintain a strong, unique password for their SSIAVerse Account and avoid sharing their authentication credentials with others.

8. Your Rights & Choices

As a SSIAVerse Account holder, you have the following choices regarding your account information:

  • Access: You may request information about the data associated with your account.
  • Correction: You may update supported profile information through your account settings.
  • Password Management: You may change your password through account settings or use the Firebase password-reset process if you forget it.
  • Deletion: You may request permanent deletion of your SSIAVerse Account and associated account data.
  • Portability: Where technically available, you may request an export of supported game progress and account-related data in a commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided in Section 12.

9. Children's Privacy

SSIAVerse games are not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

If you are under 13, please do not create or use a SSIAVerse Account without the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian where required.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information.

10. International Data Transfers

Firebase services may process or store information in locations outside your country of residence.

By using SSIAVerse Account Services, you acknowledge that your information may be processed in jurisdictions where Google Firebase operates or where its infrastructure providers maintain systems.

Google maintains security and privacy measures applicable to its services, including safeguards that may apply to international data transfers.

When we make material changes, we will update the Last Updated date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify users through in-game announcements, the SSIAVerse website, or other prominent communication channels.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically so that you remain informed about how SSIAVerse handles account information.
